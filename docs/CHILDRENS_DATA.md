# How we treat children's data

We build for children, so trust comes first. These are commitments, not features.

1. **No images, voices or names are stored.** The lamp reads the stones on the table and discards every camera frame on the device. It has no microphone.
2. **Only moves are kept.** A move is a few bytes: which stone moved, where it went, when, and whose turn it was.
3. **No cameras on faces.** A turn stone says whose turn it is. Moves made with no turn stone count for the group, never for one child.
4. **Families own the record.** A child's skill record belongs to their family, held through a data trust.
5. **Children's data is never sold.** AI labs buy tests built from our games, and results from consenting, paid **adults**. Children's data never goes to commercial AI models.
6. **Adults in the loop.** Children are only reached through the adults responsible for them: parents, teachers and club leaders.
7. **No new servers to lose.** The school's records are encrypted and split across the school's own devices, so no single phone or person can read them.
