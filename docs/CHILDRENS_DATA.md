# How we treat children's learning and data

We build for children, so these are commitments, not features.

1. **Learning comes first.** The experience has to be worthwhile for the child. Evidence exists to support the next learning decision.
2. **A child's record is never the product.** We don't sell children's data, and it never pays for the business.
3. **Records are careful.** They keep what a child did apart from what we infer from it, and note the help given, the conditions and the uncertainty. A group's success is never counted as one child's mastery.
4. **No scores or rankings of children.** There is no general intelligence score and no permanent ranking.
5. **Families can see and correct the record.** Explanations fit the child's age, and data is kept only for a stated purpose.
6. **Marketing is separate from assessment.** Taking part in a demonstration or story is a separate choice from being assessed.
7. **Adults in the loop.** Children are reached only through the adults responsible for them: parents, teachers and programme leaders.
8. **Technology earns its place.** Cameras, sensors and AI are added only where they clearly beat a simpler approach, and never to watch children.
