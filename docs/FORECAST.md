# Revenue forecast (base case)

**These are targets from our model, not results.** NBC has no revenue yet. We use the model to check that a large business is arithmetically possible, and to show what has to go right. Year 4 is not modelled. All figures are in US dollars.

## Revenue by customer

| Revenue line | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI lab testing contracts | $150K | $1,200K | $2,000K | $20,000K |
| Licences for small independent schools | $0 | $80K | $600K | $6,000K |
| Facilitator subscriptions (schools and clubs) | $6K | $75K | $540K | $5,000K |
| Pay-for-results contracts (funders, governments) | $0 | $20K | $300K | $5,000K |
| Certification of schools and centres | $0 | $0 | $150K | $3,200K |
| Kits and materials | $20K | $100K | $500K | $3,000K |
| **Total** | **$176K** | **$1,475K** | **$4,090K** | **$42,200K** |
| Blended gross margin | 57% | 59% | 61% | 62% |
| Share from AI labs | 85% | 81% | 49% | 47% |

The same numbers are in [revenue_base_case.csv](revenue_base_case.csv).

## What has to be true

| Line | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI lab contracts | 1 × $150K | 3 × $400K | 4 × $500K | 10 × $2M |
| Paid facilitators | 50 × $120 | 500 × $150 | 3,000 × $180 | 20,000 × $250 |
| Licensed small schools | – | 10 × $8K | 60 × $10K | 500 × $12K |
| Verified skills paid for | – | 5,000 × $4 | 100,000 × $3 | 2M × $2.50 |
| Certified schools and centres | – | – | 50 × $3K | 800 × $4K |

## Range

- **Year 5 low:** about $15M. School and funder lines at 40% of the base case; AI lab revenue at 30%.
- **Year 5 high:** about $68M. Every line at 160%.

## What we watch

- **Early revenue leans on AI labs.** That is our main commercial risk. From year 3 we cap AI lab revenue at half of total revenue, so the company stays an education company.
- **The first number that matters is not revenue.** It is whether children come back to the table by choice, and whether the lamp reads real stones reliably. See [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md).
- **Costs.** An operating budget will accompany the round.
