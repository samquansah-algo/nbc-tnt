# Milestones and go-to-market

*Targets and plans, not results.*

## What we will prove, and when

### Next 12 weeks
| Weeks | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | A retired phone above the table reads stamped stones live |
| 2–4 | Two game cloths, and a test of stamping codes onto local materials |
| 3–6 | The school AI running on staff phones |
| 5–8 | Tests in real classrooms: light, clutter, hands in the way |
| 8–12 | A pilot at 3–5 sites, reached through school leaders and club owners, with consent |

### By March 2027
We ask to be judged on four numbers:

1. **Capture:** the lamp reads at least 98% of stones correctly in real classrooms.
2. **Use:** 80+ children playing weekly across 3 sites. At least 60% come back by choice in week four, and teachers take no extra steps per game.
3. **Evidence:** a first published result on whether a skill carries over from one game to another, set up in advance and reported whatever it shows.
4. **Revenue:** first paid contracts, from a school or club and from an AI lab.

### By month 12
Seed-ready:
- repeatable paid pilots;
- a second country;
- an AI lab customer that comes back.

## If something fails, we change course

- **The camera can't read real stones reliably:** we switch to an NFC board with no camera at all.
- **Children don't come back:** we redesign the games before building more hardware.
- **A community doesn't want any lens above the table:** that site gets the NFC board.

## Go-to-market

| Customer | How we reach them | First step |
|---|---|---|
| After-school clubs and private schools in Cape Coast | Algo Peers' existing families, schools and paid after-school programme | 3 paid pilot sites |
| Public schools | Algo Peers' network of 31 assessed schools, funded by sponsors and grants | 1–2 sponsored sites |
| AI labs | Introductions to evaluation and safety teams; a sample test built from one game | 1 paid pilot contract |
| Funders and governments | Results from the pilot, then pay-for-results pilots | After the first published result |

**Principles:**
- Children are only ever reached through the adults responsible for them.
- We sell to schools and clubs, never directly to children.
- We grow site by site: a new site or country is added only after the last one shows children coming back and a customer paying.
